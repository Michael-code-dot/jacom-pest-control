Questions we hear a lot

How do I get a quote?

Use the quote form at the top of this page, or call or WhatsApp +254 726 700 306. Quotes are free.

Do you work with homes and businesses?

Yes. We handle residential and commercial pest control, including hotels, warehouses, offices, healthcare and pharmaceutical sites.

Which pests do you deal with?

Termites, bedbugs, cockroaches, mosquitoes, bats and rodents. Not sure what you have? Try the pest finder above.

How should I prepare before a visit?

Ask our team for advice that fits your treatment. As a general rule, keep food covered or put away, and tell the technician about children, pets and anyone with health conditions.

How do I stop pests coming back?

Prevention matters as much as treatment: seal gaps, fix leaks, store food properly and keep waste covered. Ask us about follow-up for your situation.
